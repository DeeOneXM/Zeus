# Thalanor

**A parallel digital civilization. Not a platform. Not a company. A new way of existing online.**

The internet was built without ethics baked in. Every major platform that followed inherited that absence — and built business models around it. Data became a commodity. Attention became a resource to extract. Dignity became a feature that could be turned off.

Thalanor is built on a different premise: **human dignity is a technical constraint, not a policy promise.**

This means ethics are not a layer on top of the system. They are the precondition for participating in it. The –0++ Constitutional Framework — Thalanor's governing law — operates at the code level, enforced automatically on every transaction, every commit, every interaction across the ecosystem.

## The ecosystem

Thalanor is nine integrated platforms — security, identity, value exchange, advertising, commerce, education, culture, social connection, governance — governed by a single ethical layer. Its backbone is **Aion**, the Fluid Reality Engine connecting all platforms through consent-aware data flow.

The first production-ready product is **Bashkë** — a FISK-compliant digital cooperative for small merchants, with Albania as the pilot deployment target.

The architect selection interface is at **[deeonexm.com](https://deeonexm.com)** — a puzzle, not a form.

## The scope

The –0++ Constitution does not end at national borders. Its jurisdiction extends to wherever humans communicate — including planned extraterrestrial settlements.

Mars is not an edge case. It is a test of whether a governance framework is truly civilizational, or merely terrestrial.
